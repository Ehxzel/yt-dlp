About

Strategy, built like software.

Most businesses don’t fail because they’re bad. They fail because nobody structured how the right customers were supposed to find, understand and trust them. That gap is what I fix.

I’m Abraham Adeleke, a business growth strategist. My work sits where brand, marketing and growth systems meet: three things most businesses treat as separate problems, solved by separate people.

I didn’t start in marketing. I started in software engineering, and it changed how I think about growth. Software punishes weak structure immediately. Marketing without structure just takes longer to fail.

So every business gets the same treatment I’d give a system I’m building: understand the inputs, design the architecture, then build and measure what actually moves it forward.

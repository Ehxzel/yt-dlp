About

Strategy, built like software.

I am Abraham Adeleke, a business growth strategist. My work sits at the intersection of brand, marketing and growth systems: three things most businesses treat as separate problems, handled by separate people.

I did not start in marketing. I started in software engineering, and it changed how I think about growth. Software rewards clear logic and systems that hold together under pressure. Marketing without that structure tends to produce noise: content without direction, campaigns without a thesis behind them.

So I approach every business the way I would approach a system I am building. Understand the inputs. Design the architecture. Build and measure what actually moves the business forward.

The goal is never more activity. It is a business the right customers can discover, understand, trust and choose, without friction.
